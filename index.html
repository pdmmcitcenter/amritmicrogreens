Key Nutritional Benefits

More than just a garnish, these tiny greens are packed with concentrated medicine for your body.

High Nutrient Density

Microgreens contain significantly higher concentrations of vitamins (such as A, C, E, and K), minerals (like iron, potassium, zinc, and magnesium), and antioxidants (polyphenols and beta-carotene) than their mature plant counterparts—often ranging from 4 to 40 times more concentrated.

Heart Health

Varieties like red cabbage microgreens are rich in polyphenols that help lower triglycerides and "bad" LDL cholesterol, supporting overall cardiovascular wellness.

Blood Sugar Management

Cruciferous and sulfur-rich options, such as broccoli and fenugreek microgreens, help improve insulin sensitivity and cellular sugar uptake, aiding in blood glucose control.

Brain and Cognitive Support

The high levels of antioxidants and polyphenols found in many microgreens help reduce oxidative stress in the brain and combat age-related cognitive decline.

Cancer Prevention

Brassicaceae family microgreens (like broccoli and radish) contain compounds called sulforaphane and glucosinolates, which protect cells from DNA damage and may help detoxify carcinogens.

Eye Protection

Specific varieties, particularly spinach and broccoli microgreens, supply high amounts of lutein and zeaxanthin, carotenoids known to safeguard vision and protect eyes from age-related degeneration.
